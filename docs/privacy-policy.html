Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

Overview

Oroi is a personal subscription tracking app. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data Oroi handles, how it is stored, and your rights as a user.

✦ Oroi does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with third parties. All data you enter stays exclusively on your device.

Data Stored on Your Device

Oroi stores the following information locally on your device using Android's Room database:

  • Subscription name (e.g. "Netflix")
  • Amount and currency (e.g. "14.99 €")
  • Billing cycle (weekly / monthly / annual)
  • First payment date
  • Your display name (optional, set by you)
  • App preferences (theme, language, monthly budget)

This data is stored only on your device and is never uploaded to any server.

Permissions

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — Used to send optional reminders before a subscription renews. You can decline this permission and the rest of the app will function normally.
  • INTERNET — Not requested. Oroi has no network access.

Data Sharing

Oroi does not share any data with third parties. There are no analytics SDKs, advertising networks, crash reporting services, or external APIs integrated into the app.

Data Export

Oroi includes a voluntary CSV export feature. When you use it, the app creates a local file containing your subscription list and offers you a standard Android share sheet to send it to an app of your choice. Oroi does not control what happens to the file after you share it — that is governed by the app you choose to share it with.

Children's Privacy

Oroi is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Data Deletion

All data is stored locally on your device. To delete all data, simply uninstall the app from your device. Individual subscriptions can be deleted within the app at any time.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
